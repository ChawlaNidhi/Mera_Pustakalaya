Vyanjanव्यंजन

Consonants are grouped logically by where the sound is produced in your mouth.

Ka-Varg (Velar Sounds - From the throat)

क K Like 'k' in kite
ख Kha Like 'k' with a puff of air (as in khaki)
ग Ga Like 'g' in go
घ Gha Like 'g' with a puff of air (as in ghost)
ङ Nga Nasal sound (rarely used on its own)

Cha-Varg (Velar Sounds - From the throat)

च Cha Like ch in chair
छ Chha Like 'ch' with a puff of air
ज Ja Like 'j' in joy
झ Jha Like 'jha' with a puff of air
ञ Nya Nasal sound (rarely used on its own)

Ta-Varg (Velar Sounds - From the throat)

ट Ta Like t in tomato
ठ Tha Like 't' with a puff of air
ड Da Like 'd' in dog
ढ Dha Like 'dha' with a puff of air
ण Na Retroflex n sound (tounge curled back)

Ta-Varg (Dental Sounds - Tongue touches the front teeth)

त Ta Soft t sound,Like th in thee or Spanish 't'
थ Tha Soft t with a puff of air (like th in thank you)
द Da Soft d sound (like th in them or this)
ध Dha Soft 'dha' with a puff of air
न Na Soft n sound (like n in net)

Ta-Varg (Dental Sounds - Tongue touches the front teeth)

त Ta Soft t sound,Like th in thee or Spanish 't'
थ Tha Soft t with a puff of air (like th in thank you)
द Da Soft d sound (like th in them or this)

Pa-Varg (Labial Sounds - Produced using lips)

प Pa Like p in pen
फ Pha/Fa Like f in fan
ब Ba Like b in bat
भ Bha Like bha in bhajan
म Ma Like m in mat

Antastha(Semi Vowels)

य Ya Like y in yes
र Ra/Fa Like r in ran
ल La Like l in lip
व Va/Wa Like w in was

Ushma(Sibilants/Fricatives)

श Sha Like sh in shoe
ष Like r in ran
स Sa Like s in sun
ह Ha Like h in home

Additional Retroflex Consonants/p>

ड़ Ra/Da Like soft dd
ढ़ Rha/Dha

Sanyukt Vyanjan (Compund Consonants - संयुक्त व्यंजन)) - These are 4 letters formed by combining two consonants together.

क्ष Ksha Combination of K+Sha
त्र Tra Like tr in trip
ज्ञ Gya/Jya Combination of J + Nya
श्र Shra Like shr in shred
