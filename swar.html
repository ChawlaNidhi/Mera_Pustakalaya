Swar (Vowels - स्वर)

These are independent sounds.

अ A Like 'a' in about
आ Aa Like 'a' in father
इ I Like 'i' in it
ई Ee Like 'ee' in meet
उ U Like 'u' in put
ऊ Oo Like 'oo' in boot
ऋ Ri Like 'ri' in rigid
ए Ae Like 'a' in may
ऐ Ai Like 'a' in mat or bag
ओ O Like 'o' in go
औ Au Like 'ou' in sought or bought
अं (Am / An) – Nasal sound like 'un' in under
अः (Aha) – Sound ending with a sharp breath like "ah-ha"
